# Veles Brand 3.2 — quick guide

One page. If you remember only this, you are on brand.

## The five things

1. **Colours:** Veles black `#160606` + white + one red `#ef3e46`. Never `#000000`, never another red.
2. **Font:** Manrope (free, Google Fonts) — headlines ExtraBold 800, text Light 300.
   Headlines end with a full stop: *Our work.* BR Hendrix and Arial are out.
3. **Logo:** red on black, black on red, or black on white. At least 88 px / 16 mm. Never on a photo, never in a footer.
4. **Signs:** six Veles signs (courage, power, connections, passion, creativity, integrity).
   Use rarely. Only courage may be red. No emoji icons.
5. **Layout:** a real set photo (Kadr) or clean paper with lines (Gazeta) or full red. Big type, sharp corners, lots of space.

## Banners — the new promotional look

Open `banners/banner.html` in a browser, pick the layout and size, edit the text, paste a photo URL.

- **Kadr:** set photo full bleed, Veles-black gradient from the left, white headline with a
  full stop, red logo, small pixel sign as signature.
- **Red:** full red, black logo, black headline, black courage sign cut by the edge.
- Sizes built in: LinkedIn post 1200×627, square 1080×1080, LinkedIn cover 1584×396, story 1080×1920.
