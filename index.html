01 · Why Veles

An old god, waking up.

Veles is one of the oldest Slavic gods — of the earth, the waters and the world below, of cattle and wealth, of magic and of poets. We took his name on purpose.

Everything we make is built with the newest tools — LED volumes, real-time engines, AI — and carries a root much older than us: the visual culture of Central Europe and the Slavic Balkans.

We show that root with geometry and motion, never with fake age. No parchment, no sepia, no wear.

Our circle: Poland, Slovakia, Czechia, Ukraine, Croatia, Bulgaria and their neighbours. Nordic runes and Viking styling are not part of it.

An older name. A newer technology than anything around today.

Shaping Digital Worlds for Human Future.

02 · Values

Six signs.

We named our values with signs of our own, constructed on the rules of angular Glagolitic — the script of the Baška tablet, Croatia, around 1100. They are not a historical alphabet; they are craft and intention.

Functional signs — instead of emoji.

The amulet of Veles.

Three tiers — courage + power, connections + creativity, passion + integrity — joined by diamonds on one axis, with a seal at the base. Once per document, on the cover or the last page.

Rarity is the rule.

Never a sign at every heading. A value sign once per document, two or three functional signs per page at most. Only courage may be red. Never next to the logo, never as a logo.

03 · Graphic element

The pixel sign.

Each value sign is drawn by hand on a 15×15 grid, like a cross-stitch pattern, and lit like pixels on the LED wall we shoot on — folk embroidery and Veles technology in one element.

On screens it flips its tiles in a wave from the centre and moves to the next sign every 3.6 seconds; the rail lets you choose one. It lives only in a website hero or an app start screen, at most about 200 px.

In print: one small static pixel sign as a signature, about 11 mm. Never page-size, never a pattern, never more than one live grid per screen. In text documents use the line sign.

04 · Logo

Three combinations. No more.

Red on Veles black
Black on red
Black on white

Size.

The lockup is vertical — the wordmark is about 15 % of its height. Minimum 88 px on screen, 16 mm in print headers, 32 mm on covers. No logo in footers: write Veles Productions in text.

Never.

Crop, recolour, stretch, rotate or rearrange it. Put it on a photo or a busy pattern. Add a full stop after VELES.
