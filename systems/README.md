# Veles Systems — preview only (not final)

Veles Systems is the sister brand of Veles Productions. **Its brand book is still in progress.**
This page is a preview so nobody is surprised — do not design Systems materials from it yet,
unless Artur asks.

What is already decided:

- **Same language as Productions:** the Veles signs, the pixel sign, Veles black `#160606`,
  Manrope / Alliance No.2, the same layouts.
- **One difference — the accent:** paper-cut green **`#2f9e44`** instead of red `#ef3e46`.
- **Logo: black only** — directly on green (default), or on Veles black with a green plate.
- **No white screens.** Two themes: *dark* (Veles black, green accent) and *green* (green
  background, black text and accent). Green on white is not used.
- Banners: full green field, black logo, black courage sign cut by the right edge, black text.

On the Productions website: only a small green hint (label or accent) where Veles Systems is
mentioned. The full Systems guide will come as a separate release.
