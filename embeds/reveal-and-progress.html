Reveal on scroll.

Add the class vx-reveal to any heading, paragraph, card or image. It rises 12 px and fades in once. Without JavaScript, or with reduced motion, everything is simply visible.

Light sections.

Light paper is #e7e9ed with Veles black text. The red line at the top of the window shows how far you have read.

Red breaks.

Full Veles red with black type, for section breaks and banners. Never for long text.

That is all.

No parallax, no bounce, no glow.
